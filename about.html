About Harmony Home

A personal service built on two decades of expertise, a genuine love for order, and deep care for every home we work in.

Harmony Home was founded by Renata Cintra, a personal home organiser and mindful cleaner with over 20 years of experience in the cleaning and home organisation industry. Based in Bristol, Renata brings warmth, professionalism, and an exceptional eye for detail to every home she works in.

Her philosophy is simple: a well-kept, thoughtfully organised home creates a feeling of calm and wellbeing for the whole family. Clutter and disorder quietly weigh on us — and the opposite is equally true. When a home is clean and harmonious, life genuinely feels lighter.

Every client is different, and so is every home. Renata takes the time to understand how you live, what matters most to you, and how your space can better serve your daily life. The result is always personal, never generic.

My goal is to help families live in spaces that feel harmonious, orderly, and peaceful — a home that truly supports the life they want to live. — Renata Cintra

At Harmony Home, we also offer eco-friendly cleaning products for clients who care about sustainability. Respecting your home and the environment goes hand in hand — and high quality should never come at a cost to either.
